# Chrome Bridge

Lets DSH work in the Chrome you already use: real clicks and typing, a cursor you can see on the page, and your own logins.

- Pages it opens go into a separate window, so your own window gets no new tabs and keeps focus.
- It never touches the page you are looking at on its own.
- Pause it any time: click the DSH Bridge icon in Chrome's toolbar and turn off "Let DSH control the browser".

## Install

1. In DSH, open **Plugins**, click **Add plugin**, enter `dsh-chrome-bridge`, then restart DSH.
2. Add the DSH Bridge extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bippcbbbipppmhjkjomgdnjbooecbimo).

Once both are in, the plugin shows "Connected" on the Plugins page.

## Update

Open it on the Plugins page and click "Update to …". A new version can be installed about a day after it is released.

## Languages

- The extension panel in Chrome follows Chrome's language: English, Chinese or Russian.
- The settings card on the Plugins page follows DSH's language.
- To give the model English or Russian tool descriptions too, see "Tool descriptions in English or Russian" in the [full guide](https://github.com/OrderG-X/dsh-chrome-bridge#-tool-descriptions-in-english-or-russian).

## Requirements

macOS, Windows or Linux, and Chrome 116 or newer.

Full guide: https://github.com/OrderG-X/dsh-chrome-bridge
